### Moon Mochi art direction

One upright plum pouch as the clear hero; cream MOCHI lettering, a small crescent mark, restrained lavender orbit lines, one cut mochi. Matte flat shapes, softly layered paper tones and ample breathing room. No piles of food, hands or extra props.

### Mori art direction

One large phone with a few readable interface elements, one sage growth curve, and a translucent five-icon glass navigation capsule. Editorial title to the left; one concentric circle and a single organic arc carry the motion. Warm porcelain and quiet greens, with open space instead of extra screens or tiny dashboard detail.
